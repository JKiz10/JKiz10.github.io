A Cambria Cover and Eleven More Houston Interior Design Features

Twelve features across trade press, national platforms, a podcast and one manufacturer cover. Each entry names the publication, says what the piece was actually about, and links out so you can read it rather than take our word for it.

Several of these came looking for a specific detail in a specific room, which is the kind of coverage we would rather have than a general profile.

Features by year

2026
Houzz Magazine

10 Bathroom Storage Ideas From Best of Houzz 2026 Award Winners

Houzz went to its 2026 award winners for storage that earns its space, and took a small Houston bathroom of ours. The detail they singled out was the frameless mirrored medicine cabinet with integrated LED, which adds storage and bounces light in a room that had neither to spare.

Read it on Houzz
2025
Cambria Style, Issue 28

Spring 2025 cover story

Cambria publishes Cambria Style for its own quartz surfaces, and one project takes the cover each season. Spring 2025 went to a full renovation of a three-story condo, worked up against a prewar New York penthouse reference. Written by Eloise Goldman, photographed by Gisele Parra.

Open the Spring 2025 issue  See the Vue Point project
2023
Business of Home

The Best of Houzz awards showcase the platform’s most creative and service-oriented stars

Published 4 April 2023, on the firms that had won Best of Houzz repeatedly. Jennifer is quoted by name. By then the record stood at five Design awards and six straight years of Service.

Read the article
2023
Tile Club

Designing Functional Spaces With a Touch of Warmth

Published 14 August 2023 by Lindsay Fluckiger. An interview about a Moroccan-inspired bathroom built around Spanish Siesta mosaic, and about where a Veracruz upbringing shows up in a Texas house.

Read the interview
2023
Tile Trends x Tile Club, podcast

Jennifer Kizzee’s Design Vision: From Mexico to Your Home

Recorded 11 August 2023, about twenty minutes. Jennifer walks through the Moroccan-style bathroom retreat and how a Mexican upbringing turns into a material palette. The written Tile Club interview three days later came out of this conversation.

Listen to the episode
2023
The Metropolitan Builder

Dialogue with a Designer

August 2023, Greater Houston edition, pages 16 to 20, in the series sponsored by MWG Innovations. On founding the studio, and on what has changed in the trade since 2016. The line the magazine led with was that she founded the firm the same way she came to America, with a vision and a lot of determination.

Read the reprint, PDF
2021
Houzz Magazine

New This Week: 4 Fresh Midsize Bathrooms With a Low-Curb Shower

Written by Mitchell Parker, published 8 October 2021. The room is our own kids’ bathroom in League City, fifty square feet, five by ten. Slate blue four-by-twelve tile stacked, matte porcelain geometric floor running up over the curb, brass and champagne bronze fixtures, Sherwin-Williams Tricorn Black.

Read it on Houzz

Dialogue with a Designer

May 2021, Greater Houston edition, pages 12 to 14, in the series sponsored by Baths of America. The first of two interviews the magazine ran with Jennifer. This one covers the design journey and the material and labor shortages the trade was living through that year.
