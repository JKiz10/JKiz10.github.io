Fifteen Best of Houzz Awards for Houston Interior Design

Houzz gives two separate awards each year and they measure different things. Design is decided by how the community responds to a firm’s portfolio. Service is decided by client review volume and rating. We have won at least one of them every year since 2018, and both in six of those years.

Below is the full record, year by year, with what each award is given for and where to check it. Nothing here is a logo. Every line is a claim you can verify.

What the two awards actually measure

Design and Service are not the same award

The Design award goes to firms whose portfolios drew the most engagement from the Houzz community over the year. It is a read on the work itself, not on the client experience behind it.

The Service award goes to firms rated highly by clients across a meaningful volume of reviews in the same period. A studio can design beautifully and still lose it.

We list them separately because collapsing them into one number hides which one a firm actually won. Six Design awards and nine Service awards is a more useful sentence than fifteen awards.
