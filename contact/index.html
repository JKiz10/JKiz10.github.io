Before you write

What makes a project a fit

Most of our work is large in scope. A kitchen and primary suite taken back to the studs. A whole home reworked room by room. A new build where we sit with the builder before the slab is poured. On a renovation of that size, Jennifer typically spends at least a year with one client.

We handle the drawings, the selections, the bidding, the procurement and the install, so you make decisions instead of phone calls. If that is the kind of help you are looking for, we should talk.

We are selective about what we take on, and we would rather tell you early that we are not the right studio than take a job that does not suit either of us.
