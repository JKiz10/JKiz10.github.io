Cambria Style · Spring 2025

The cover story

Cambria publishes its own magazine for its quartz surfaces, and one project takes the cover each season. Spring 2025 went to Gateway Condo, a full renovation of a three-story condo in The Woodlands, worked up against a prewar New York penthouse reference.

The kitchen on the cover sets black cabinetry and a brass-banded hood against a walnut island, white marble counters and a checkerboard marble floor.

Founder and Principal Designer

Jennifer Kizzee

Jennifer founded the firm in 2016 after a career in sales and marketing, and she still leads every project that comes through the studio. She grew up on the coast in Veracruz, and that shows up in the work: warm materials, real color, rooms that are built to be lived in rather than looked at.

The team is nine people now. Designers, a procurement manager, and the systems to keep a single kitchen and a whole-home build running at the same time without either one slipping.
