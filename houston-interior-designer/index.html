The short version

What full service actually means

There are firms that hand you a mood board and wish you luck. That is not what this is.

Full service means we carry the project end to end. We measure and draw the space. We select every finish, fixture, tile, cabinet and paint color. We build the bid package your contractor prices from, so the numbers you get back are comparing the same thing. We place and track every furniture order, receive it, inspect it, and hold it until install day. Then we install the whole house at once, so you walk into a finished room instead of watching boxes arrive for four months.

The point is not that we do more. It is that you make design decisions and we handle everything downstream of them.

How a project runs

Our process

Nine steps, the same order every time. Knowing what comes next is most of what makes a renovation bearable.

Design Consultation

We come to the house, walk the space, and talk scope, timeline and budget honestly. You leave with a clear read on the project whether or not you hire us.

Contractual Phase

Scope, deliverables and terms in writing before any design work starts. No surprises about what is included.

Schematics

Measurements, space planning and the first layouts. This is where walls move on paper instead of in real life.

Design Refining

Materials, finishes, cabinetry, lighting and furniture come together into one coherent scheme rather than a pile of good individual choices.

Design Presentation

You see the whole design at once. Drawings, samples, elevations, the actual pieces. You approve it before anyone orders anything.

Project Bidding

We build the bid package your contractors price from, so every bid is quoting the same scope and you can compare them honestly.

Procurement

Every order placed, tracked, received and inspected. Damage gets caught in our warehouse, not in your living room.

Implementation & Project Management

Site meetings, trade coordination, and answering the questions that come up mid-build so nobody guesses at a detail.

Install Day

Everything goes in at once. You leave in the morning and come back to a finished house.

Before you call

Common questions

What does a full-service interior designer actually do?

Full service means we carry the project from the first measurement to the day the last piece is placed. That covers space planning and drawings, every finish and fixture selection, the bid package your contractor prices from, ordering and tracking all the furniture and materials, and managing the install. You are not sourcing tile on a Saturday or chasing a backordered vanity. You approve the design and we run it.

Do you work in Houston if your studio is in League City?

Yes. Most of our work is inside the Houston metro. The studio sits in League City because that is where Jennifer lives, but our projects run from the Museum District and the Heights out through The Woodlands, Pearland, Friendswood, Clear Lake and the Galveston corridor.

How early should I bring in an interior designer on a new construction home?

Before the slab if you can, and no later than framing. The decisions that matter most are structural: where the windows land, how the kitchen is laid out, whether the primary bath has room for the layout you want. Those get locked in early and get expensive to change later. We regularly sit down with builders during plan review.

Do you take on kitchen or bathroom projects on their own?

Yes, and a lot of our best-known work is exactly that. A single kitchen or a primary bath taken down to the studs is a real project with real drawings, real selections and real construction coordination. It gets the same process a whole home gets.

What is the first step in working together?

A paid design consultation. We come to the house, walk the space, and talk through scope, timeline and budget honestly. You leave that meeting with a clear read on what your project involves whether or not you hire us. If it is a fit, we build a proposal from what we learned.

Do you work with our builder or contractor?

We do, and we prefer it. We produce a bid package your builder can price accurately, we sit in on site meetings, and we handle the questions that come up mid-construction so your builder is not guessing at a finish detail. We also work regularly with builders who bring us in on their own projects.

Do you handle furniture and accessories, or only the construction side?

Both. Furnishings and procurement are part of full service. We specify the pieces, place and track the orders, receive and inspect them, and install everything at once so the room is finished the day you see it rather than arriving in boxes over four months.

What kind of projects are the best fit?

Large-scale residential work. Whole-home renovations, major gut projects, and new construction interiors where we are involved from the plans forward. We onboard selectively because full service means real hours on every project, and we would rather give more time to fewer clients.

How long does a Houston renovation take?

Design usually runs two to four months before anything is demolished, depending on scope and how quickly selections get approved. Construction is set by your builder and by permitting. A single kitchen or bath is often three to five months end to end. A whole home is commonly nine to eighteen months. Lead times on custom pieces drive more of that schedule than people expect, which is why we specify early.

Are you actually award-winning, or is that just a phrase?

Fifteen Best of Houzz awards across Design and Service, every year from 2018 through 2026. Jennifer and the firm have been featured by Business of Home, Houzz Magazine, Metropolitan Builder and Tile Club, and the studio was on the cover of Cambria Style in Spring 2025. Every one of those is listed with its year and its source on our Recognition page.
