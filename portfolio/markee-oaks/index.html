Markee Oaks

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Markee Oaks

Navy runs the island and the lower cabinetry, with black granite on top and a soft greige on the perimeter so the room does not close in. White cone pendants lined with gold hang the length of the island.

The bar is the moment. An arched niche lined in black herringbone tile, pale oak shelves floating across it, and a blue and white granite counter underneath with the wine storage below.
