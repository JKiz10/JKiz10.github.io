League City, TX · Kitchen renovation

Longwood

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Longwood

Charcoal cabinetry the length of the room, with black granite over it and a white herringbone tile behind the range. The island ends in a slab of reclaimed wood, which keeps the dark from going flat.

Brass lanterns hang above it, and brass returns on the pulls, some of them wrapped in walnut. The tall wall holds the pantry, the ovens and a pair of glass front beverage fridges.
