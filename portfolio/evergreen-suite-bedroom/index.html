Evergreen Suite Bedroom

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Evergreen Suite Bedroom

A floor-to-ceiling navy grid runs wall to wall behind the bed, applied as a squared lattice and returning at the corner. Against it the palette holds to four colours and stops: navy, rust, camel, cream.

A brindle hide bench on brass legs sits at the foot. The black steel and frosted-glass barn door is the same door language as the bathroom next door, hung here on an exposed black track.
