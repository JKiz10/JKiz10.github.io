Kitchen and primary bath renovation

Bayou End

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Bayou End

Stained white oak runs floor to ceiling, broken by a black hood banded with rivets and a stacked tile backsplash that catches the light. The island is a mitred marble waterfall on a fluted base, with four cane-back stools under three aged brass pendants. A glass-fronted cabinet with black mullions holds the ceramics, and the drawers below it are reeded.

The primary bath goes the other way. The vanities are plum and reeded with brass knobs, under marble counters, one of them cut with a shaped backsplash. The shower is framed in brass and glazed like a window, tiled floor to ceiling in octagon and dot, with a marble bench, a two-tier niche and a window that stays obscured.
