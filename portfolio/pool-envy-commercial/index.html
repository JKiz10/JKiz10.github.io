Pool Envy

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Pool Envy

A selection room has one job: make choosing easy. We put the samples on lit racks along one wall, the screens where everyone can see them, and a black table in the middle with stools that people actually stay in.

Navy carries the branding wall, a wood slat wall warms the room, and brass sconces and dome pendants light it without the glare a showroom usually has.
