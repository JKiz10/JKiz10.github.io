The Woodlands, TX · Primary bath renovation

Crescent Bath

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Crescent Bath

The paper set the room. A blue and white pattern runs the full height above the wainscot, and the walnut vanity underneath is carved rather than plain, which keeps the room from reading new.

Marble counters, polished nickel fittings and arched mirrors between the sconces. The tub sits under the window on its own, with a tile niche beside it and beadboard storage running to the ceiling.
