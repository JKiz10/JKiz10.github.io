Takoma Park

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Takoma Park

Our one project outside Texas, and worth showing for what it does with colour. A deep green wet bar anchors the open lower level, with a brass bottle shelf and rail on a dark figured back wall under two dome pendants.

Upstairs the two seating rooms take different fireplaces: one a dark stone surround between arched wood niches, the other a linear firebox set in a full field of green and white quatrefoil tile. A blue library carries brass picture lights across its case fronts.

This is the only project in the portfolio outside the Houston area. We travel for the right job.
