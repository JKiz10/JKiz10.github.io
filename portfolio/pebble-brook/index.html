Pebble Brook

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Pebble Brook

Dark stained cabinetry runs the island and the hood wall, with white uppers above and marble behind the range. A pair of arched steel and glass doors closes the kitchen off from the hall without closing off the light.

The dining room carries the same wood into built-ins with a lit arch, green upholstered chairs around a black oval table. The laundry and powder room go further: teal cabinetry, a travel print paper, and a dark floral over green subway tile.
