Kitchen and powder room renovation

Evergreen Kitchen

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Evergreen Kitchen

White oak carries the kitchen and the white perimeter stays out of its way. A shaped oak hood sits over the range against a veined stone slab with a brass pot filler set into it, and the island below runs the same stone on a raised panel oak base. Tall oak cabinets flank a black steel window at the sink, the pantry door is cut with a scalloped arch, and a drawer beside the range is fitted in oak to hold the spices flat.

The rooms either side of it are where the house speaks up. The laundry is tiled in dark stacked tile over a basketweave floor, with a marble lined recess for the dog bowls and a swing arm filler above them. The powder room is panelled navy below and papered above in a blue and white Houston toile, the skyline and a Westheimer sign among the pumpjacks and longhorns, lit by white globe sconces either side of a gold framed mirror.
