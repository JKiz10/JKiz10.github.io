Houston, TX · Kitchen renovation

Pinewold Kitchen

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Pinewold Kitchen

One room, done thoroughly. The island is the whole idea: a heavily veined grey and white slab with warm mineral streaking, mitred into a full waterfall at the seating end, with the cooktop set into it.

Two brass lantern pendants with rings of clear glass rods hang above. The perimeter runs white raised-panel cabinetry with applied mitred moulding, and a pearled handmade tile backsplash carries up to three walnut shelves on brass brackets.
