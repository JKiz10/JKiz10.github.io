League City, TX · Bathroom and closet renovation

Rockpoint

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Rockpoint

Two rooms, taken together. The bathroom runs the full length of the space, from a glass shower past a freestanding tub to a double vanity wall in navy grasscloth. Three pierced metal Moroccan lanterns hang against textured white plaster.

The tub niche and the shower niches are lined in the same multicolored mosaic, blue and terracotta and ochre against cream. The closet picks up the navy on an island base topped in marble, under a black iron lantern.
