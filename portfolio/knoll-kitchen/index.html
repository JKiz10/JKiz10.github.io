Friendswood, TX · Partial home renovation

Knoll Kitchen

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Knoll Kitchen

A partial home renovation that centres on the kitchen. A painted plaster hood with a curved apron sits over a stainless pro range with brass knobs, against a full-height marble slab that runs its veining unbroken across the wall.

Two woven brass drum pendants hang over the island. The coffee bar niche breaks the white with sage glazed brick under white oak floating shelves, and a corner sitting nook picks up cane and plantation shutters.
