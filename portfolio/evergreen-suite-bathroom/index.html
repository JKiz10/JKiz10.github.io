Houston, TX · Bathroom renovation

Evergreen Suite Bathroom

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Evergreen Suite Bathroom

Sage green palm wallpaper sets the tone and everything else stays quiet against it. The vanity is two-tone, a white oak drawer bank between grey painted towers, under wall-mount brass faucets with four-lobed clover handles.

The shower uses a calacatta marble hexagon as a niche back and an accent band. A black steel frosted-glass door connects the room to the bedroom next door, which is the detail that ties the suite together.
