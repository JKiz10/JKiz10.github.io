Katy, TX · Primary bath renovation

Sunstream Bath

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Sunstream Bath

A capiz shell chandelier hangs over the slipper tub, and everything else in the room is tuned to it. The vanity is dusty blue, soft and grey-leaning, and it takes white ceramic knobs rather than pulls, which is the choice that makes the room.

Champagne bronze runs throughout on cross-handle faucets and the shower fittings. A reeded walnut linen tower breaks the blue, and the floor is laid in greige herringbone.
