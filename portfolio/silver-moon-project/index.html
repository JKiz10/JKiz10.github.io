Silver Moon

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Silver Moon

One room, and a small one, treated as though it mattered. An L-shaped bench is built into the window corner with working storage drawers underneath, and the walls are horizontal shiplap in a dusty slate blue.

A black iron cage lantern hangs over a round pedestal table on a jute rug. The floor is dark stained plank, which is what makes the blue read the way it does. The windows look out onto a lake.
