Crystal Beach Vacation Home

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Crystal Beach Vacation Home

A full renovation and furnishing on the Bolivar Peninsula. The great room sits under a cathedral ceiling in white shiplap, with a staircase on a stainless cable rail.

Colour does the work here. The kitchen is denim blue with ribbed glass and brass pendants. The guest bath runs turquoise fish-scale tile up the vanity wall and into the shower. The hall bath stripes white subway with an aqua liner over pale blue board and batten.
