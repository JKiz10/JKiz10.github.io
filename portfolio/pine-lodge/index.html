Pine Lodge

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Pine Lodge

Four baths, each given its own character and held together by the same hand. The primary runs white oak against a black stone slab, with black sconces and gold fittings and a patterned floor.

From there the rooms lighten. A navy vanity under floral paper, a pink zellige shower with round niches, and a craft room in lilac with built-in desks against a green floral wall.
