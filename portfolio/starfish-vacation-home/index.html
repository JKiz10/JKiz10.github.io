Starfish Vacation Home

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Starfish Vacation Home

An elevated house on pilings with two levels of covered porch. The plan sleeps a crowd: four bedrooms, a bunk room, three full baths, a den and a built-in office, all of it worked around white oak and a blue and white palette.

The kitchen sets a heavily veined blue granite island against a herringbone backsplash. The primary bath is lined in navy glazed subway tile with a teak bench. The bunk room is papered in blue fish, with built-in berths on both walls.
