The Woodlands, TX · Complete home renovation

Vue Point

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Vue Point

We took this house on three levels and reworked the whole interior, room by room. The kitchen carries twin waterfall islands under paired black slat pendants. The living room is built around a charcoal grid-tile fireplace wall that runs floor to ceiling, flanked by arched niches of white oak shelving.

The material story is consistent from floor to floor: warm brass against cool charcoal, white oak against navy, and marble used where it will be seen up close. The kitchen backsplash is inlaid with brass ovals. The dining wall pairs navy cabinetry with a gold herringbone panel.

This project ran as the cover story of Cambria Style, Spring 2025. See the press page.
