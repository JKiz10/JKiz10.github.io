Friendswood, TX · Bathroom renovation

Mountain Falls Primary Bath

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Mountain Falls Primary Bath

The room is symmetrical and it earns it. A freestanding tub sits dead centre under a beaded crystal chandelier, framed by white oak ceiling beams, with a slate grey vanity to each side.

The tub wall is the signature material: white marble and weathered taupe stone in a woven pinwheel mosaic under a white oak ledge. The metals are deliberately mixed, brushed nickel on the plumbing and brass on the cabinet hardware, mirrors and shower glass.
