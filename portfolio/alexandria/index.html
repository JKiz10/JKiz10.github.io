Friendswood, TX · Whole home renovation

Alexandria

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Alexandria

We took this house room by room and let white oak carry the millwork through it. The kitchen runs a marble island under black cone pendants hung from a brass frame, with a brass-strapped hood over the range and a full wall of oak pantry doors beside it.

The smaller rooms hold the color. The laundry is sage green over a checkerboard floor, the powder room pairs a checked paper with a gilt mirror, and the living room sits against a stone fireplace under exposed beams.
