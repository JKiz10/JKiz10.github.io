Bolivar Beach House

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Bolivar Beach House

Blue green tile runs the full height of the kitchen behind a stainless range, under a wood hood and a plank ceiling that follows the roof line. The island is white quartz on a walnut base, with rattan stools pulled up to it and brass globe pendants overhead. Open shelves in the corner hold the everyday dishes.

The living room keeps the same ceiling and adds teal swivel chairs around a round walnut table. Both bedrooms are papered, one in a palm print. The bunk room is built into blue grey cabinetry with the beds set into the wall and a desk that folds down out of it. Laundry hides behind cane fronted doors. The baths carry green and blue tile, fluted wood vanities and brass.
