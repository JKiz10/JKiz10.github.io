Marrelli Project

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Marrelli Project

Three baths and a bedroom, worked so they belong to one house. Gold framed mirrors and brushed gold fittings repeat from room to room, and walnut shelving warms the grays and navies.

In the primary bedroom we ran a navy board and batten wall the full width behind a black four-poster bed, then kept the sitting area pale so the window wall stays the brightest thing in the room.
