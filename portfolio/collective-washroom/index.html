League City, TX · Washroom suite

Collective Washroom

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Collective Washroom

Two rooms, two moods, one material language. The first runs a marble slab wall behind twin oval mirrors and a stone trough, with white oak drawers and fluted storage holding the line underneath.

The second turns dark. A scalloped tile wall wraps behind a curved oak vanity and a black stone vessel sink, and a fluted black door closes it off from the hall.
