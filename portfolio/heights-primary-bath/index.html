Houston, TX · Primary bath renovation

Heights Primary Bath

Home  ·  Portfolio  ·  Heights Primary Bath

The house is old and we kept the bath that way. A dark floral paper runs above white subway wainscot, the floor is hex mosaic with a black dot border, and the tub is a clawfoot sitting on its own.

The vanity is walnut with a marble top, cross handle faucets in polished nickel, and glass knobs on the doors. Fluted milk glass shades on brass swing arms light the mirrors.
