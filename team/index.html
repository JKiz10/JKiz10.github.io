The Houston Interior Design Team Behind Every Project

A studio of nine, and a collection of women with backgrounds in design, corporate and marketing. On a whole-home renovation you will work with more than one of us, so it is worth knowing who does what.

Designers carry the drawings and the selections. Procurement carries the orders, the freight and the install date. Jennifer carries the project.

The people at Jennifer Kizzee Design

Founder and Principal Designer

Jennifer Kizzee

Jennifer started the studio in 2016 after a career in sales and marketing, and she still leads every project that comes through it. She takes the first conversation on a new job herself.

More about Jennifer

Senior Interior Designer

Paige Haynes

Paige has more than twenty years in design, remodeling and new construction, which makes her our most tenured designer. She runs the drawing and the schedule at the same time, which is rarer than it sounds.

Procurement Manager

Jennifer Schmidt

Jennifer manages procurement logistics on every project, from a background in finance and years working with large home builders. She is the reason the install date holds.

Interior Designer

McKayla Gookin

McKayla brings a harmonious, slightly whimsical hand to a room. Her remodeling experience lets her carry several medium to full-size renovations at once without dropping any of them.

Interior Designer

Sydney came to design through art, and it shows in the layered, detailed rooms she puts together. She takes on our most intricate projects and most of our vacation homes.

How we work together

One project, more than one set of hands

A large renovation runs for a year or more. Over that stretch the drawings, the selections, the bids, the orders and the install all need someone whose actual job it is. Splitting those roles is what keeps a project moving when a cabinet shipment slips or a wall opens up on something nobody expected.
