Greater Houston

Where We Design, Across Greater Houston

The studio sits in League City, between Houston and Galveston. We work from the Woodlands down to the Bolivar Peninsula, and west to Katy.

The six cities below have their own page because projects were actually built there. Everywhere else we serve is listed underneath, honestly, without a page pretending to be more than a service area.

Inside the Loop

The Heights, River Oaks, Memorial and West U

Most of our Houston enquiries come from inside the Loop and the Memorial corridor. The Heights has its own page, built around the period restoration on this site, in an old house we kept old on purpose.

We take work across River Oaks, Memorial, West University Place, Bellaire, Tanglewood and the Museum District. The Houston page lists the neighbourhoods and the six projects behind them.

North and west

The Woodlands, Katy and Cinco Ranch

The Woodlands is our second-largest body of work after League City: four projects, two of them whole houses taken room by room. Carlton Woods, Creekside Park and Hughes Landing are all the same drive.

Katy and Cinco Ranch are the far side of the metro and we take work there anyway. Sugar Land, Fulshear and the Energy Corridor are the same run.
