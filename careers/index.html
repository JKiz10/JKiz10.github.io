Careers at a Houston Interior Design Studio

We are nine people in League City. Designers, a procurement manager, and the systems that let a kitchen and a whole-home build run at the same time without either one slipping.

We hire slowly and rarely, and we keep people. Our most tenured designer has more than twenty years in design, remodeling and new construction.

What the work is

Large projects, carried end to end

Most of our work is a year or more with one client. Whole homes taken back to the studs, primary suites rebuilt around a single idea, new builds where we are at the table with the builder before the slab is poured.

A designer here draws the millwork, runs the selections, reads the bids, and is on site through the install. It is not a role where you hand a scheme over and move on.
