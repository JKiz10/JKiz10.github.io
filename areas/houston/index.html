Interior Design in Houston

Houston is where most of our enquiries come from and where six of the projects in this portfolio were built. We drive in from League City, close enough that site visits are routine rather than an event.

The Houston work runs from a single kitchen to a primary suite taken as two rooms at once. Pinewold is built around a mitred waterfall island with the cooktop set into it. The Heights Primary Bath is a period restoration in an old house, kept old on purpose.

Where in Houston we work

Houston is not one market. A bungalow in the Heights and a high-rise off Holcombe want different drawings, different trades and different budgets, and we have done both.

We work across the city and inside the Loop in particular. Listing a neighbourhood here means we take work there, not that there is a finished project on this page for every name.

What we take on

Scope, and how we work

Most of our work is large. Whole homes taken back to the studs, primary suites rebuilt around one idea, kitchens where the millwork is drawn before the cabinets are ordered. We also take single rooms when the room deserves it.

The drawings, the selections, the bidding, the procurement and the install all sit inside the firm, so you make decisions instead of phone calls. The nine stages are here.
