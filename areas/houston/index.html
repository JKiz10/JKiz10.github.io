Houston, Texas

Interior Design in Houston

Houston is where most of our enquiries come from and where three of the projects in this portfolio were built. We drive in from League City, which is close enough that site visits are routine rather than an event.

The Houston work in the portfolio runs from a single kitchen to a primary suite taken as two rooms at once. Pinewold is built around a mitred waterfall island with the cooktop set into it. The Evergreen Suite is a bathroom and bedroom tied together by the same black steel and frosted-glass door.

Scope, and how we work
