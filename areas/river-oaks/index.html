Before you begin

What a River Oaks renovation asks for first

Before anything is built in River Oaks, the Architectural Review Committee has to approve it. Not just a new structure: additions, pools, fences, driveways, drainage, landscaping and solar panels all go through the same door. The submission is architectural plans, elevations, site layouts and landscaping, and the restrictions reach as far as how much of the lot can be covered in hard surface.

Some River Oaks houses are also City of Houston Landmarks or Protected Landmarks, and exterior work on those can need a Certificate of Appropriateness on top of the Committee.

This is the part most homeowners underestimate, and it is the part a full set of construction documents exists to answer. We draw the elevations, specify the finishes and prepare the document set the builder prices from, so what goes to review is the design as designed rather than a sketch of it.
