Friendswood, Texas

Interior Design in Friendswood

Friendswood is fifteen minutes from the studio and one of the areas we work in most consistently. The houses here tend to be larger and older than the coastal stock, which usually means a renovation rather than a refresh.

Both Friendswood projects in the portfolio are single-room jobs done thoroughly. Knoll Kitchen sets a painted plaster hood against a bookmatched marble slab. Mountain Falls is a primary bath built around a beaded chandelier over the tub, under white oak ceiling beams.
