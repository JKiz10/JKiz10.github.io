Interior Design in the Houston Heights

The Heights Primary Bath is the project on this page. The house is old and we kept the bath that way, which is the whole brief in one sentence.

A dark floral paper runs above white subway wainscot, the floor is hex mosaic with a black dot border, and the tub is a clawfoot sitting on its own. The vanity is walnut with a marble top, cross handle faucets in polished nickel and glass knobs on the doors.

What we take on

Scope, and how we work

Most of our work is large. Whole homes taken back to the studs, primary suites rebuilt around one idea, kitchens where the millwork is drawn before the cabinets are ordered. We also take single rooms when the room deserves it.

The drawings, the selections, the bidding, the procurement and the install all sit inside the firm, so you make decisions instead of phone calls. The nine stages are here.
