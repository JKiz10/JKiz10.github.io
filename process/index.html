How a project runs

Nine Stages From First Call to Install Day

A whole-home renovation usually takes a year or more, and knowing its shape up front is what keeps it manageable.

Every stage of our process ends with a clear deliverable and a decision point, so you always know what you're approving and what comes next.

The nine stages

Discovery and Consultation

Start with a complimentary discovery call then book a paid on-site consultation if it's a fit.

Contractual Phase

Scope, deliverables and timeline go in writing before any drawing starts. Nobody should be guessing at what is included.

Schematics

First plans. Layouts, elevations, where walls move and where they cannot. This is the stage where the expensive ideas get tested cheaply, on paper.

Design Development

This is where the home gains its character. We source and present the materials, finishes, and fixtures, compare samples in person, and refine each choice until the full palette feels effortless.

Design Presentation

The full scheme in one sitting. Drawings, selections, materials in hand. You see the room before it exists, and you approve it before anyone orders anything.

Project Bidding

Once the design is approved, we prepare a complete set of construction documents and entrust them to the builder. As your liaison, we guide them through every detail so the design is carried out exactly as intended, and the pricing you receive reflects the scope as designed.

Procurement

Your dedicated procurement manager personally orders and tracks every furnishing and finish through final delivery. Should freight damage, a backorder or a substitution arise, it is managed on your behalf and brought to you only when a decision is needed.

Implementation and Project Management

We carry the design from drawings to reality through consistent site visits, close coordination with your builder, and steady communication that keeps every detail aligned with the vision.

Install Day

Furniture, art, styling, and the walkthrough. The house is finished, photographed and handed back to you.

Why it is structured this way

You make decisions instead of phone calls

The drawings, the selections, the bidding, the procurement and the install all sit inside the firm. That is what full service means here, and it is the reason the stages above look the way they do.

Splitting the roles is what keeps a project moving when a cabinet shipment slips or a wall opens up on something nobody expected. Designers carry the drawings and the selections. Procurement carries the orders, the freight and the install date.
